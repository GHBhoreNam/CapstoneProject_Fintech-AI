# CapstoneProject_Fintech-AI

. Headline Layer Interpretation

Total GMV represents the overall payment volume processed during the analysis period. Success rate indicates the percentage of transactions that were successfully captured and settled. The reconciliation match rate measures operational data quality by verifying whether transactions agree across both the ledger and gateway exports. The chargeback ratio provides an indicator of dispute activity and fraud risk across the platform.

2. Trends Layer Interpretation

Daily GMV highlights variations in customer spending throughout the 30‑day period and helps identify peak transaction dates. Daily chargeback counts can be compared against GMV trends to determine whether increases in volume are associated with higher dispute rates. Sudden spikes in chargebacks may indicate fraud events, operational issues, or merchant-specific problems requiring investigation.

3. Breakdown Layer Interpretation

The payment method chart reveals which channels contribute the largest share of transaction value and can help guide payment-optimization efforts. The merchant category chart identifies business segments driving platform revenue. Categories with high GMV but elevated chargeback exposure may merit enhanced monitoring and risk controls.

4. Details Layer Interpretation

The top‑merchant table identifies the merchants generating the highest transaction volumes. The chargeback ratio is calculated at merchant level using transaction counts rather than transaction amounts. Any merchant whose chargeback ratio exceeds 1% is flagged for review, enabling risk teams to prioritize merchants that may require additional compliance, fraud, or operational monitoring.
